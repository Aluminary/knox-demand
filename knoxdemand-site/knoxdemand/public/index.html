Questions People Ask

Straight answers

What does a demand generation company do?+

A demand generation company builds the systems that turn local awareness into booked customers: SEO and content so people find you, fast-response tools like live chat and a 24/7 receptionist so leads don't go cold, and follow-up systems like CRM, review requests, and referral programs so past customers come back and bring others with them.

How much does it cost?+

One flat package priced at $5,000 total, covering SEO, lead generation, a 24/7 receptionist, live chatbot, CRM, review and loyalty systems, and hiring support. Only 5 new client spots open each week, starting with a free strategy call.

What's the difference between SEO and demand generation?+

SEO is one channel that helps people find your business in search. Demand generation is the full system around it — getting found, getting contacted quickly, getting booked, and getting customers to come back and refer others.

Do you only work with businesses in Knoxville?+

Knox Demand is based in and built around the Knoxville, TN market, with local pages structured for the whole metro area. The same system can extend to additional cities as a business grows.

How fast will I see results?+

The 24/7 receptionist and live chatbot can start recovering missed calls and messages in the first week. SEO and review growth build over one to three months as content, citations, and reviews accumulate.

Why does review management matter?+

Review count and recency influence local search visibility, and reviews are what a new customer reads before calling. Review Cards make it easy for happy customers to leave one right after service.
